## Observations

I like Costar as an application. It makes me feel connected with my girlfriend who loves stars and drawing meaning out of them.

As soon as you open the application, you see "Your day at a Glance". In broad letters: "You will love who you want, whenever you want." It follows with: "Stop waiting for perfect conditions to fall in love. …"

### Do and Don’ts

Three Dos and three Don’ts.

What I like: it makes the user open the app to see what it is. The app focuses on personalization and knows that it is being shared with a person, so perhaps that is tailored toward the relationship.

**Lesson:** immediately create and deliver value. Make the user return and let them access a bit of it easily.

### Dive Deeper

A "Dive Deeper" CTA prompts a user to join Pro-Star. It includes:

- Decode your day at a glance to see how it plays out in real life.
- Daily recommendations for what to read, watch, and listen to, based on your astrology.
- Be the first to know when full moons and retrogrades are coming.
- The lessons the stars want you to learn today.

### Life areas

- Luck in sex and love
- Luck in work and money
- Changes in home and family
- Changes with friends

### Friend updates

A friend update can say that a partner is dating someone whose love language is words of affirmation. It makes the product feel connective and creates an air of mystery.

### Compatibility report

The feature answers questions such as:

- How does the other person see me?
- What do they want?
- Is it meant to be?
- Why are they playing games?
- Will it work out?

The product promises insight into how good two people are together using both natal charts. It can address long-term potential, communication styles, physical attraction, and emotional connection with scales or ratings.

## Transferable product mechanics

1. Give a concise, personalized, emotionally salient insight immediately on open.
2. Use depth—not the initial aha moment—as the natural premium boundary.
3. Create a daily reason to return through new readings and meaningful events.
4. Make relationships a private, consent-aware source of value rather than an engagement feed.
5. Answer uncertain, emotionally important questions without claiming unwarranted certainty.
